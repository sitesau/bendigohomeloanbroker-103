Maximise First Home Buyer Incentives

Navigating grants and guarantees can be complex. The Victorian First Home Owner Grant provides a lump sum for eligible new homes, while the federal First Home Guarantee allows a deposit as low as five percent without paying lenders mortgage insurance. However, price caps for these schemes are reviewed annually and differ based on location. It is vital to check these thresholds against the median prices of areas like Kangaroo Flat or Strathdale to ensure your target property qualifies before you commit.

Understand the Broker's Role

A common misconception is that using a broker adds cost to your loan. In the Australian residential market, brokers are typically paid commission by the lender, not the borrower. This service is usually free for you. Since January 2021, brokers are legally required to act in your best interests under the ASIC RG 273 guidelines. They must prioritise your needs and present options that are suitable for your specific situation rather than the products that pay them the highest commission.

Structuring Loans for Future Goals

When buying, consider your five to ten year plan. An offset account can be a powerful tool for reducing interest on an owner occupied loan, while interest only periods might suit investors focusing on cash flow. Refinancing later to access equity for renovations or investment is a common strategy. A broker can help you select a loan now that offers the flexibility you might need later, avoiding potentially expensive switch fees down the track.

Common questions

Do I pay a fee to use a mortgage broker in Bendigo? Most residential mortgage brokers in Australia do not charge the borrower a fee. They are typically paid commission by the lender, which must be disclosed to you upfront.

Can I buy a home with a 5 percent deposit? Yes, if you are eligible for the First Home Guarantee. This scheme allows eligible first home buyers to purchase with a 5 percent deposit without paying lenders mortgage insurance, subject to property price caps.

How long is pre-approval valid for? Conditional pre-approval is typically valid for around 90 days. If you have not found a property within this time, you may need to update your financial information and renew the approval.
